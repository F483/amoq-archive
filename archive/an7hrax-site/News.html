..::LvL posts Storm Sector 7 review.
October 2, 2002
  Yesterday Storm Sector 7 was reviewed and submitted on LvL, the review actually turned out to be better then I expected it to be.
  You can find the review here.
  Yes I eventual took the time to make the site and considering the fact that I'm a total newbie in webdesign, I think the site turned out
  quite good ;-). So your probably asking yourself "What's this all about?", well I'm an amateur Level Designer and make levels (also
  called maps) for computer games. Right know I'm focusing on Quake III Arena and my main project is a 1v1 map called Storm
  Sector 7, which is getting along well and will be reaching beta status soon. I've also started work on TDM map but its not even
  near alpha and will take some time to be finished. Later this week I'll upload a plan update so you can see what I'm doing.

January 28, 2002
  Its been a while since I've done anything, but after a "short" break ;-), I eventually took up the work on Storm Sector 7 and its almost
  finished. Today I made the first official beta which I will be testing with some close friends to find weak spots or bugs. So the map
  should be up sometime next week if everything goes right.
  Storm Sector 7 almost finished.

July 1, 2002
  Storm Sector 7 is finally finished and it took long enough. The gameplay is just fine thanks to the help of Hellz and Koloss, who tested it
  with me and gave me advice. I used some textures and models from Evil Lair and Mr.Clean which give it a nice look, thanks to some
  technical tips from Chaos. I've also released the map source for Storm Sector 7 under the ..::LvL Open Game Source License. The Map,
  a description, info's and screenshots are in my Maps section, the download is here. The source is in my Files section or here. Have Fun!
