## The leagues

**GRAL — German Rocket Arena League**, one of three leagues run by *The Leagues*
(`the-leagues.de` → `the-leagues.com`) by Phoenix and JarJarBinks, alongside **ERA2L**
(European Rocket Arena 2) and **ERA3L** (European Rocket Arena 3). AMOQ played the GRAL
season of autumn 2000 and withdrew from it on 26 October 2000.

**ESPL** turned out to be the **Electronic Sports League** (`espl-europe.net`) — the league
that later became ESL. That is where AMOQ carried on as **Riedbauern** in RA3 2on2 and TDM
2on2, ranked 11th in RA3 in February 2001.

Neither league's 2000 data was archived. The Internet Archive's first capture of The Leagues'
database is April 2001, five months after AMOQ left, and AMOQ is not in it — not in the clan
database, not in any table. The GRAL tables that *were* captured are Liga 1 only; AMOQ played
in a lower division.

### Where the opponents ended up

From the April 2001 tables (saved in [`../archive/opponents/the-leagues/`](../archive/opponents/the-leagues/)):

- **Suck My Rocket** — 4th in **GRAL Liga 1**, the top German division. They beat AMOQ 316:119.
- **Unmatched** — 7th in **ERA2L Liga 1**, the top European RA2 division. They beat AMOQ 22:2.

So the two worst hidings AMOQ took were from clans that were, or became, top-flight.

## The one opponent-side record that survives

**HellBlade** (`hellblade.gnw.de` — hosted by GNW, the same provider as AMOQ) kept their own
wars table, and it includes the AMOQ match:

| Date | Opponent | Type | W/L | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 03.09.2000 | amoq | cw | won | **3:1 (maps); 17:16 (rounds)** |

This is the match AMOQ's own news reported the same day as *"Clanwar vs. HBC … Wir haben knapp
17:16 verloren"* — HBC and HellBlade are the same clan. Their table adds the detail AMOQ's
never recorded: the **map score, 3:1**.

Their page linked a per-match report at `hellblade.gnw.de/ra3/cw/amoq.htm`. It was never
archived. Saved copies of what did survive: [`../archive/opponents/hellblade/`](../archive/opponents/hellblade/).
